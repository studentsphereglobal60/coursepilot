About CoursePilot

Make your learning choice clearer.

CoursePilot is an independent course discovery and learning-guidance project for a worldwide audience. Our goal is to explain who a course may suit, what to compare before enrolling, and which skills to consider next. We are not a course provider and do not guarantee jobs, income or certificates.
